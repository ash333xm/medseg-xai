USP 1: Transformer Multi-Modal & Multi-Layer Relevance Explainability (TMME) Chefer et al. (CVPR / ICCV 2021)
Standard raw attention maps suffer from background noise and lack class specificity. TMME calculates gradient-weighted attention relevance filtered by ReLU and rolls it out across transformer layers.
Mathematical Formulation of TMME Relevance:
A_bar^{(l)} = (1 / H) * Σ_{h=1}^H max(0, A_h^{(l)} ⊙ ∇_{A_h^{(l)}} L)
R^{(l)} = R^{(l-1)} + A_bar^{(l)} · R^{(l-1)}, with base condition R^{(0)} = I_{N×N}
Where A_h^{(l)} is the multi-head attention map, ∇ L is the gradient of the lesion segmentation loss, ⊙ is Hadamard elementwise multiplication, and max(0, ·) is ReLU activation isolating positive relevance.

HIPAA Safe Harbor Compliance Framework MEDSEG-GOV-2026-V1
Clinical neuro-oncology data governance adhering to 45 CFR § 164.514(b)(2) and GDPR.

1. Safe Harbor De-Identification Protocol

All clinical DICOM studies have undergone complete automated redaction of all eighteen (18) Protected Health Information (PHI) identifiers: names, dates, geographic data, MRNs, biometric markers, and serial numbers.

2. Zero-VRAM PACS Isolation Architecture

- No External Network Leakage: All patient pixel arrays are processed client-side or within isolated container boundaries.
- Stateless Execution: Patient volumes are purged upon session termination.
- Zero Model Mutation: Weights are read-only; no backpropagation or parameter updates occur during audit.

Research Use Only (RUO). This software is NOT approved by the FDA or EMA for primary clinical diagnosis, surgical navigation, or treatment planning.
