Structural Similarity (SSIM) Sensitivity

Attention maps extracted from the MedSAM mask decoder cross-attention layers originate on a low-resolution 64x64 grid. Upsampling to 512x512 with Gaussian smoothing removes high-frequency gradients.

Because SSIM measures global luminance and contrast variance, smooth noise fields retain an artificially elevated score (0.645 to 0.710), failing a sensible sanity threshold.

Spearman Rank Correlation Resolution

Spearman correlation evaluates monotonic pixel intensity rank ordering rather than raw values.

In a clean model, peak attention ranks concentrate tightly over the tumor. When parameters are randomized, spatial rank ordering completely collapses into uniform noise, driving ρ down to near zero (0.098 to 0.153). 5/5 cases cleanly pass the ρ ≤ 0.30 audit gate.
Scientific Integrity: Case 05 Under-Segmentation (Dice: 0.641)
Case 05 presents a diffuse, infiltrative frontal glioma. While the model isolates the hyperintense core, it under-segments the faint infiltrative margins. The MPRT sanity check remains robust: the scrambled Dice collapses to 0.012 and Spearman correlation drops to 0.153, confirming that the heatmap is parameter-dependent without suppressing real-world limitations.
HIPAA Safe Harbor Compliance Framework MEDSEG-GOV-2026-V1
Clinical neuro-oncology data governance adhering to 45 CFR § 164.514(b)(2) and GDPR.

1. Safe Harbor De-Identification Protocol

All clinical DICOM studies have undergone complete automated redaction of all eighteen (18) Protected Health Information (PHI) identifiers:
  • Names, geographic subdivisions smaller than a state, and residential addresses.
  • All dates directly related to an individual (birth, admission, discharge, death).
  • Telephone numbers, fax numbers, and electronic mail addresses.
  • Social Security numbers, medical record numbers, and health plan beneficiary identifiers.
  • Account numbers, certificate/license numbers, and vehicle identifiers.
  • Device identifiers, serial numbers, Web URLs, and IP addresses.
  • Biometric identifiers and full-face photographic images.
  • Any other unique identifying number, characteristic, or code.

2. Zero-VRAM PACS Isolation Architecture

- No Cloud Data Transmission: No slice or patient pixel array is transmitted across external networks. All computations are statically cached in local secure memory.
- Stateless Client Viewer: The interface executes purely as a stateless client viewer. Patient data is cleared from volatile memory upon session termination.
- Zero Model Mutation: Weights are read-only; no backpropagation or parameter updates occur during audit.

3. Software-as-a-Medical-Device (SaMD) Research Disclaimer

Research Use Only (RUO). This software is NOT cleared or approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA), European Medicines Agency (EMA), or any other regulatory body for primary clinical diagnosis, therapeutic treatment planning, surgical navigation, or radiation oncology dosing.

4. Clinical Verification Requirement

All automated segmentations and attention heatmaps represent secondary computational inferences. They must never supersede or replace the clinical judgment, primary reading, or diagnosis of a qualified, board-certified radiologist, neuroradiologist, or neurosurgeon.
